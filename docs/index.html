Privacy Policy

Brain Bee Prep — Last updated: March 20, 2026

Overview

Brain Bee Prep ("the App") is an educational study tool designed to help students prepare for Brain Bee neuroscience competitions. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect (spoiler: virtually none) and how we handle it.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App operates entirely offline on your device. Specifically:

  • No account creation or login is required
  • No personal information (name, email, phone number) is collected
  • No location data is accessed
  • No photos, contacts, or files on your device are accessed
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used
  • No analytics or crash reporting services are integrated

Local Data Storage

The App stores your study progress (e.g., quiz scores, flashcard progress, bookmarks) locally on your device using standard device storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the App

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms. There are no in-app purchases, ads, or external links that track your activity.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we are compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Since the App does not collect data, we do not anticipate significant changes.
